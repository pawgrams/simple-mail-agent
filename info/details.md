# 3. Role Descriptions

## Frontend Developers

Frontend Developers are responsible for the **client-side development of web and application interfaces**. Their work focuses on translating design concepts into functional user interfaces that run in web browsers or mobile environments.

Typical technologies include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks such as React, Angular, or Vue.js. Frontend developers collaborate closely with UI/UX designers and backend engineers to ensure a seamless user experience.

Their responsibilities often include optimizing application performance, ensuring cross-browser compatibility, and implementing responsive design for different devices.

---

## Backend Developers

Backend Developers focus on the **server-side logic of applications**, including databases, APIs, and application architecture. Their work ensures that applications process data efficiently and securely.

Common programming languages used in backend development include Java, Python, C#, Node.js, Go, and PHP.

Backend developers build the infrastructure that powers modern applications, handling tasks such as authentication systems, database queries, microservices architecture, and integration with external services.

---

## Full-Stack Developers

Full-Stack Developers possess expertise in both frontend and backend technologies. They are capable of developing **complete application systems**, from user interfaces to backend infrastructure.

These professionals are particularly valuable in smaller teams and startup environments where engineers must work across multiple layers of the technology stack.

Full-stack developers often work with frameworks such as Node.js, Django, Spring Boot, or Ruby on Rails combined with frontend frameworks like React or Vue.

---

## DevOps Engineers

DevOps Engineers specialize in **automation, infrastructure management, and continuous deployment pipelines**.

Their primary goal is to bridge the gap between software development and IT operations by implementing automated workflows for building, testing, and deploying applications.

DevOps professionals typically work with technologies such as Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, and cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.

Their work helps organizations achieve faster release cycles and more reliable system operations.

---

## Software Architects

Software Architects are senior technical leaders responsible for **designing the overall architecture of complex software systems**.

They make strategic decisions regarding technology stacks, scalability strategies, system integrations, and long-term maintainability.

Software architects often work closely with engineering teams, product managers, and business stakeholders to ensure that technical solutions align with business goals.

---

## Software Testers / QA Engineers

Software Testers and QA Engineers ensure that applications meet quality standards before release.

Their responsibilities include developing **automated and manual test cases**, identifying bugs, and verifying that software behaves as expected.

Modern QA engineers frequently use automated testing frameworks such as Selenium, Cypress, or Playwright to create continuous testing pipelines.

---

## UI/UX Designers

UI/UX Designers focus on the **user experience and interface design** of digital products.

They conduct user research, design wireframes and prototypes, and create visually appealing interfaces that are intuitive and easy to use.

Their work ensures that digital products are not only functional but also accessible and user-friendly.

---

## Tech Product Owners

Tech Product Owners act as the **link between technical teams and business stakeholders**.

They define product requirements, manage development backlogs, and prioritize features according to business value.

Product owners work closely with agile development teams to ensure that projects move forward efficiently and align with user needs.

---

## IT Project Managers

IT Project Managers coordinate complex technology initiatives within organizations.

Their responsibilities include **project planning, resource allocation, risk management, and stakeholder communication**.

They ensure that software development projects are delivered on time, within budget, and according to defined requirements.

---

## Cloud Engineers

Cloud Engineers design, implement, and maintain **cloud-based infrastructure systems**.

They work with platforms such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud to build scalable and reliable infrastructure environments.

Their tasks often include cloud architecture design, infrastructure automation, cost optimization, and security management.

---

## Prompt Engineers / AI Specialists

Prompt Engineers and AI Specialists focus on **working with large language models and artificial intelligence systems**.

They design prompts, workflows, and system integrations that enable organizations to leverage AI technologies effectively.

This role has emerged alongside the rapid growth of generative AI and machine learning applications.

---

## Data Analysts

Data Analysts focus on interpreting structured data to help organizations make informed decisions.

They typically work with tools such as SQL, Python, Excel, or business intelligence platforms like Tableau or Power BI.

Their work includes data visualization, reporting, and identifying trends within datasets.

---

## Data Scientists

Data Scientists work on advanced data modeling, machine learning, and predictive analytics.

They develop algorithms that can identify patterns within large datasets and generate forecasts or automated decision systems.

Typical tools include Python, R, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and various data engineering frameworks.

---

## IT Consultants

IT Consultants advise organizations on technology strategy, system implementation, and digital transformation initiatives.

They often work on projects involving cloud migration, infrastructure modernization, cybersecurity strategies, and enterprise software integration.

IT consultants combine technical expertise with business strategy to help organizations improve efficiency and competitiveness.

# 6. Market Environment of IT Recruitment

The global demand for software engineers and digital specialists continues to grow as organizations across all industries digitize their operations.

This demand has created a **significant talent shortage in many technology disciplines**, particularly in software engineering, cloud infrastructure, and data science.

Recruitment agencies play a crucial role in connecting companies with highly specialized professionals.

Fees for recruitment services are typically calculated as a percentage of the candidate's annual salary. In many European markets this fee ranges between **15% and 30% of the annual salary**.

Factors that influence pricing include:

- seniority of the role  
- scarcity of skills  
- urgency of the hiring process  
- exclusivity of the search mandate  
- geographic market conditions  

Highly specialized or executive roles may command higher placement fees.
